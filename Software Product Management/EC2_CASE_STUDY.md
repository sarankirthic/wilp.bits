# CASE STUDY 1 — FitTrack

### Background

HealthNova is a software company that traditionally develops healthcare applications for hospitals. 
The company wants to create a new SaaS product called **FitTrack**, a wellness and health-monitoring 
platform for corporate employees.

The initial internal proposal includes:

* Daily health tracking
* Goal setting
* Exercise tracking
* Diet tracking
* AI health recommendations
* Wearable integration
* Doctor consultation
* Health reports
* Gamification
* Social/community features

The engineering team believes that all features should be included because competitors offer similar functionality.

However, discussions with potential customers reveal that HR teams are mainly interested in **employee wellness participation and aggregate reporting**, while employees want **simple tracking and reminders**. Employees are not particularly interested in complex AI recommendations at this stage.

The company has limited engineering capacity and wants to launch within four months.

---

## Q1. Product vs Project — 5 Marks

FitTrack initially started as an internal initiative to demonstrate the company's technical capabilities.

### a)

Explain the difference between developing FitTrack as a **project** versus developing it as a **product**.

### b)

Based on the case, explain what characteristics would indicate that FitTrack should be treated as a product.

---

## Q2. Customer, User and Buyer — 7 Marks

FitTrack has several stakeholders:

* Employees
* HR managers
* Company management
* IT administrators
* Healthcare partners

### a)

Identify the likely **users, customers and buyers**.

### b)

Explain how their needs differ.

### c)

Why should the Product Manager distinguish between these groups when defining the product?

---

## Q3. Problem vs Feature — 8 Marks

The engineering team proposes:

> “Let's build AI health recommendations because competitors have them.”

However, customer discussions suggest that employees mainly struggle with remembering to track their activities consistently.

### a)

Identify the **actual customer problem** indicated by the case.

### b)

Explain why the proposed AI recommendation feature may not necessarily be the correct first solution.

### c)

Suggest two possible solutions to the underlying problem.

---

## Q4. MVP — 10 Marks

The team has only four months and limited engineering resources.

From the following features:

* Daily tracking
* Goal setting
* Reminders
* AI recommendations
* Wearable integration
* Doctor consultation
* Gamification
* Community
* HR dashboard

### a)

Propose a suitable **MVP**.

### b)

Explain the criteria you used to decide what belongs in the MVP.

### c)

Identify two features you would deliberately postpone and explain why.

# CASE STUDY 2 — QuickCart

### Background

QuickCart is a startup developing an online grocery application.

The company has received the following feature requests:

| Feature                 | Customer demand | Business value   | Development effort |
| ----------------------- | --------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Search                  | Very High       | High             | Low                |
| Online payment          | Very High       | Very High        | Medium             |
| Loyalty points          | Medium          | Medium           | Medium             |
| AI recommendations      | Low             | Potentially High | Very High          |
| Order tracking          | High            | High             | Medium             |
| Social sharing          | Low             | Low              | Medium             |
| Multiple delivery slots | High            | High             | High               |

The engineering team can implement only **three major features** in the next release.

---

## Q1. Feature Prioritisation — 10 Marks

### a)

Explain the factors you would consider when prioritising these features.

### b)

Prioritise the features for the next release.

### c)

Explain the reasoning behind your prioritisation.

**Don't simply say “high demand = first.” Explain trade-offs.**

---

## Q2. Product Roadmap — 8 Marks

Create a simple roadmap for QuickCart using:

```text
NOW → NEXT → LATER
```

Explain why the features have been sequenced this way.

---

## Q3. Product Backlog vs Roadmap — 6 Marks

QuickCart currently has 80 feature requests and engineering tasks in its backlog.

The CEO asks:

> “Why can't we simply show the backlog to investors as our product roadmap?”

Explain the difference between:

* Product roadmap
* Product backlog

and explain what information each communicates.

---

## Q4. Release Planning — 6 Marks

The team discovers that online payment depends on a third-party payment provider that will be available only one month later.

How should the Product Manager respond?

Discuss:

* Dependencies
* Prioritisation
* Release planning
* Roadmap adjustment

# CASE STUDY 3 — LearnPro

### Background

LearnPro is an online learning platform used by college students.

The product team discovers that students frequently complain:

> “There are too many courses and I don't know which one to choose.”

The team proposes:

> “Let's build an AI course recommendation engine.”

However, the PM wants to investigate the problem before committing engineering resources.

The team conducts interviews with 30 students and discovers that students mainly struggle because:

* Course descriptions are unclear.
* Prerequisites are difficult to understand.
* Students don't know which courses fit their career goals.
* There are too many similar courses.

---

## Q1. Product Discovery — 8 Marks

### a)

Explain why the PM should perform **product discovery before product delivery**.

### b)

Based on the research, identify the key customer problem.

### c)

Explain how the research changed the team's understanding of the problem.

---

## Q2. Product Thinking — 8 Marks

The engineering team argues:

> “We already know the solution: AI recommendations.”

Explain how a **product-thinking approach** would handle this situation.

Your answer should distinguish:

* Problem
* Need
* Solution
* Feature

---

## Q3. User Story & Acceptance Criteria — 8 Marks

Based on the case:

### a)

Write one suitable **user story**.

### b)

Write at least **four acceptance criteria** for that user story.

---

## Q4. MVP — 6 Marks

The team has two options:

**Option A**

Build a complete AI recommendation system.

**Option B**

Start with a simpler course-selection assistant using:

* Career goal
* Prerequisites
* Course difficulty
* Course category

Which approach better fits an MVP? Explain why.

# CASE STUDY 4 — TravelMate

### Background

TravelMate is developing a travel-planning application.

The company has identified three customer groups:

1. Individual travellers
2. Corporate travellers
3. Travel agencies

During research:

* Individual travellers want simple itinerary planning.
* Corporate travellers want expense tracking and company policy compliance.
* Travel agencies want white-label functionality.
* Management wants revenue quickly.
* Engineering can support only one major customer segment initially.

The PM proposes targeting individual travellers first.

---

## Q1. Customer & Market Understanding — 7 Marks

### a)

Identify the different customer/user groups.

### b)

Explain why their needs differ.

### c)

What additional research should the PM perform before selecting the initial target segment?

---

## Q2. Early Adopter — 7 Marks

TravelMate wants to identify early adopters.

Explain the characteristics of a **good early adopter**.

Then explain which of the three segments could potentially be suitable as an early adopter and why.

---

## Q3. Product-Market Fit — 8 Marks

After launch, TravelMate gets:

* 50,000 downloads
* 5,000 registrations
* 500 active monthly users
* 100 paying users

The CEO claims:

> “We have achieved product-market fit because we have 50,000 downloads.”

### a)

Do downloads alone establish product-market fit?

### b)

What additional evidence should the PM examine?

### c)

What metrics could help understand whether the product is providing sustained value?

---

## Q4. Product Success — 8 Marks

The team releases a new itinerary feature.

The PM reports:

> “The feature is successful because development finished on time.”

Explain the difference between:

* **Output**
* **Outcome**

Then propose suitable outcome measures for the itinerary feature.

* Business value

# 🧠 What These Papers Are REALLY Testing

Looking at the actual EC-2 paper you uploaded, the examiner is testing whether you can take a concept and **apply it to a messy real-world situation**.

The first paper, for example, doesn't ask:

> “What is an early adopter?”

It gives you **three segments** and asks you to determine which is appropriate and justify it.

It doesn't simply ask:

> “What is MVP?”

It gives a company risks and asks how it can **use an MVP to reduce risk**.

That pattern is consistent with case-study style product-management questions: product cases commonly test problem identification, user understanding, MVP decisions, prioritisation, roadmap thinking and trade-offs rather than pure definitions. ([Duke Career Hub][1])

### 🚨 One very important thing from your actual paper

The marking instructions say **25–35% can depend on CRISPNESS**.

So don't prepare only the *content*. Prepare the **answer format** too:

```text
CASE FACT
   ↓
CONCEPT
   ↓
APPLICATION
   ↓
JUSTIFICATION
   ↓
CONCLUSION
```

For a 7-mark question, for example, don't write a giant essay.

Use:

```text
1. Recommendation
2. Evidence from case
3. Relevant concept
4. Application
5. Justification
```

That is much closer to what your actual paper is demanding.
